* Real user accounts

4. Server-side authorization

Implement reusable middleware or authorization services.

Commissioner:

* Full access to teams, rosters, schedules, scores, and review queues
* Can assign or replace captains
* Cannot exceed the roster limit

Captain:

* Can manage only their assigned team
* Can submit scores only for games involving their team
* Can confirm or dispute scores submitted by the opposing team
* Cannot change finalized scores
* Cannot resolve disputes as commissioner

Player:

* Can view authorized league information
* Can update only their own basic profile
* Cannot manage rosters or scores

All authorization must occur in Express handlers or shared backend services, not only in the interface.

5. Functional roster management

Implement working API endpoints and UI actions for:

* List teams
* Create team
* Rename team
* Activate or deactivate team
* Assign or replace captain
* View a team roster
* Invite player
* Copy invitation link
* Resend or regenerate an invitation
* Cancel invitation
* Remove player
* Accept invitation
* Update personal profile

Roster rules:

* Maximum occupied roster size is 8.
* Active memberships and pending invitations both occupy roster positions.
* Commissioner cannot bypass the limit.
* Enforce the limit inside a PostgreSQL transaction with appropriate locking so simultaneous requests cannot create a ninth position.
* Canceling or expiring an invitation opens a position.
* Removing a player opens a position.
* Invitation tokens must be cryptographically random.
* Store only a secure hash of the invitation token.
* Tokens must expire and be single-use.
* Only an authenticated account with the invited normalized email may accept the invitation.
* Invitation acceptance and membership creation must be atomic.
* No temporary players, placeholder players, or jersey numbers.

Actual outbound email is not required for this beta. Generate a secure copyable invitation link and keep email delivery behind a replaceable service interface.

6. Functional scheduling

Replace the hardcoded schedule with persistent commissioner-managed games.

Commissioner must be able to:

* Create and edit venues
* Create and edit courts
* Create a game manually
* Edit a draft or published game
* Publish a game
* Cancel a game
* Filter games by team and date

Players and captains may view only published, pending, disputed, final, or cancelled games. Draft games are commissioner-only.

Reject:

* Home team equal to away team
* Inactive teams
* Inactive venues or courts
* A team scheduled in two games at the same time
* A court scheduled for two games at the same time
* A game outside the active season
* Invalid dates or times

Perform conflict validation server-side and make concurrent conflicting requests safe.

7. Functional score workflow

Implement these transitions:

PUBLISHED → PENDING_CONFIRMATION
PENDING_CONFIRMATION → FINAL
PENDING_CONFIRMATION → DISPUTED
DISPUTED → FINAL through commissioner resolution

Rules:

* A captain may submit a score only for a published game involving their team.
* The opposing captain may confirm or dispute it.
* The submitting captain cannot confirm their own score.
* A dispute requires a reason.
* Commissioner may submit, correct, finalize, or resolve any score.
* Final scores are locked from captain editing.
* Commissioner corrections to final scores require an audit event.
* Reject negative, non-integer, missing, or otherwise invalid scores.
* Store actor and timestamp information for every transition.
* Write an AuditEvent for submission, confirmation, dispute, resolution, and commissioner correction.

Build a working commissioner review queue for pending and disputed scores.
